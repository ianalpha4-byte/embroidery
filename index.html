About Us

Ann's Interior Decors – Ann's Embroidery Centre

Ann's Interior Decors – Ann's Embroidery Centre is a home décor shop located at Flyover (Wakimbo) along Nairobi–Naivasha Highway, Kenya. We specialize in high-quality curtains, carpets, pillows, curtain rods, duvets, bedsheets, and sofa loose covers. We offer stylish and affordable interior solutions for homes in the region and surrounding areas.

Our mission is to help you create a warm, comfortable, and beautiful living space. Whether you're redecorating a single room or transforming your entire home, we have the perfect products to suit your taste and budget.
